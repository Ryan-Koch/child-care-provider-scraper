Child Care Provider Search

Use the search options below to search for State registered Family Day Care programs; State licensed child or school age care programs; or Informal or In-Home (those who care for one family) providers in South Dakota communities.

  • After making your selections using search criteria, a provider list will generate.
  • Select an individual provider to view the provider’s profile, inspections, compliance plans, corrective action plans, or a stipulation consent agreement.

Note: If your child care provider is an informal provider or in-home provider (cares only for your children), you can view their inspection and compliance information by entering their provider ID number below. Please contact the local Child Care Licensing staff. Local phone numbers are found on the following website: https://dss.sd.gov/childcare/licensing/districts.aspx.
