3. Child-Staff Ratio
3. Child-Staff Ratio:
Child to staff ratio must be adhered to during all hours of operation. Ratios are determined by a point system. Points are determined by the age of children. Twelve points per staff member is the maximum. Child 0-24 mos. = 2 pts.; Child 24-36 mos. = 1.5 pts.; Child 36-60 mos. = 1 pts.; Child 60 mos-13yrs = .5 pts. Local jurisdictions that license child care providers may be more stringent.
March 20th, 2026
Incident Category Supervision Concern

Supervision Concerns

Provider self-reported that a baby had rolled off the changing table. The health department performed an investigation and determined that a staff member was not actively supervising an infant and therefore the infant was able to roll off the changing table. 

Resolution

The staff member in question was immediately terminated and the provider added some boards on the side of the changing table to minimize the potential fall/rolling-off risk.

December 10th, 2025
Incident Category Child Abuse and Neglect

Child Abuse from Staff Member

The provider self-reported a child may have been bitten by a staff member. During investigation, it was verified that a child was bitten and it was further escalated to law enforcement. 

Resolution

The staff member in question was terminated and law enforcement pursued charges for Injury to a Child.
